Privacy Notice

{# Release 7: set to the deploy day. #}

Last updated: 19 November 2026

KuickKonvert ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website KuickKonvert.com, providing file conversion and PDF utility tools. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website.

What we do with your files

Files you upload to KuickKonvert are used only to perform the conversion you requested. Each upload is processed in its own temporary, isolated workspace on our server -- including any working files the converter creates along the way -- and our application automatically deletes that workspace as soon as your converted file is returned to you, or if the conversion fails. If a server process stops unexpectedly in the middle of a conversion, any workspace it leaves behind is removed automatically by a later clean-up. We do not manually review your uploaded files or retain them for purposes unrelated to providing the conversion service. Temporary copies and working files may be created automatically as part of the conversion process and are deleted through our file-cleanup procedures. This deletion is carried out by our application; we cannot guarantee that no trace remains at every layer of our hosting provider’s infrastructure, such as storage hardware or backups that Render manages under its own policies.

What we don't do

Our application does not keep your files after conversion. We do not use uploaded file contents to train any model. We do not sell your uploaded files or disclose their contents to third parties for their independent commercial purposes. Our hosting and network service providers process data as necessary to operate and secure the service (see “Our service providers and where data is processed”).

Technical logs and IP addresses

Like any website, our server and our hosting provider see your IP address and basic request details -- such as the page or tool requested, the time and the response code -- in order to deliver pages and protect the service. We use IP addresses to apply fair-use limits on how many pages and conversions can be requested per minute, per hour and per day; these counters are held in memory for no longer than 24 hours. Our own application logs record which tool was used and technical error details when a conversion fails. They never contain file contents, and they are designed to leave out file names, although a report of an unexpected error may occasionally include one. These application logs are kept in our hosting provider’s log service, which on our current Render plan keeps them for 7 days. Cloudflare and Render may also keep network and request logs, including IP addresses, for their own security and operational purposes under their own retention policies.

Our service providers and where data is processed

  • Render (Render Services, Inc., United States) hosts KuickKonvert. Our server runs in Render’s Singapore (Southeast Asia) region, and your uploaded files are converted there and deleted as described above. Render also receives the technical request information and application logs described in this notice.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., United States) provides the network that Render uses to deliver and protect traffic to our server. All website traffic, including the files you upload, travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections through Cloudflare’s network, usually through a Cloudflare data centre near you, before it reaches our server. To do this, Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical information about your requests, and your uploaded files pass through its network in transit.

This means your file and request information may be processed in countries other than your own, including Singapore, where our server runs, the countries where Cloudflare’s network handles your traffic, and the United States, where Render and Cloudflare are based. Render’s Data Processing Addendum, which supplements its Terms of Service, provides that transfers of personal data from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are made under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension or the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or, where those do not apply, under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, and it requires Render to bind its sub-processors, including Cloudflare, to comparable data-protection obligations. Render and Cloudflare are both listed as active participants in those frameworks (Data Privacy Framework list, checked 13 November 2026). Participation in the Data Privacy Framework covers transfers to those companies in the United States; it does not by itself cover processing in other countries such as Singapore. We require our service providers to maintain appropriate contractual and other safeguards for international processing and onward transfers, as required by applicable data-protection law. We remain responsible for ensuring that our arrangements with these providers provide an appropriate level of protection for personal data. Google is described separately below.

1. Cookies and Similar Storage

KuickKonvert’s own functional code does not set any tracking cookies. However, when you visit our site, our consent management system and third-party measurement tools (such as Google Analytics and Google Ads) may place cookies or local-storage entries on your device, provided you have given appropriate consent.

Your Consent Choices

We store your preferences regarding analytics and advertising cookies in your browser’s local storage under the key kk_consent. This record contains:

  • Your choice for Analytics (accepted or rejected)
  • Your choice for Advertising (accepted or rejected)
  • The date and time your choice was recorded
  • The version of the consent message accepted

This consent record stays entirely in your browser and is not transmitted to us.

2. Google Analytics

If you consent to Analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve our conversion tools and user experience.

Automatically Generated Events: Google Analytics may record automatically generated events, such as page views, scrolling to a point near the end of a page, and user engagement. The specific events collected depend on our Google Analytics configuration.

Tool & Form Interactions: GA4 may track custom interactions to help us measure tool utility (such as when a tool page is viewed or when a conversion utility is initiated).

What our analytics events leave out: Our Google Analytics events are designed not to include your uploaded files, their file names or their contents, document text or values you type. We test our event payloads for this information, and our tests have not found it in them.

3. Google Ads

If you consent to Advertising, we use Google Ads tags to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns and understand how visitors arrive at KuickKonvert.

Ad-Click Identifiers: Page addresses and titles may include ad-click identifiers (such as gclid) when you arrive via an advertisement.

Remarketing & Personalisation: When permitted by your consent settings, Google Ads may utilize remarketing signals to deliver relevant advertising experiences.

Conversion Tracking: We use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure conversions attributed to eligible interactions with our ads, according to configured attribution settings.

4. Visitors in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other Consent-Controlled Locations

If you are accessing KuickKonvert from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another Consent-Controlled Location (defined below), no Google tags or third-party measurement scripts will load until you explicitly provide your consent via our consent banner.

Consent States & Behavior

  • No Choice Yet / Reject All: Google tags are not loaded, and no Google network requests are sent.
  • Analytics Only: Google Analytics is loaded to measure site performance; advertising storage and remarketing remain disabled.
  • Advertising Only: Google Ads tags are loaded; analytics collection remains inactive.
  • Accept All: Both categories are enabled according to your preferences.

We will ask for your preferences again after 12 months, or sooner if we materially update our consent management practices or processing categories.

Withdrawing or Changing Your Consent

You can change your choice at any time by clicking Cookie settings in the footer of any page.

If you withdraw consent for Analytics or Advertising:

  • We update your saved choice in local storage and remove the relevant cookies and storage entries that our site is able to clear.
  • We reload the page where necessary so that the corresponding tags stop executing immediately.
  • The category for which you have withdrawn consent will not be loaded on subsequent pages. Any category you continue to allow may remain active according to your updated preferences.

Please note: Our site cannot remove information that Google stores under its own domains or information Google has already received prior to withdrawal.

5. Third-Party Content (LaunchNest Badge)

For privacy and performance:

  • For visitors in the Consent-Controlled Locations, we display a plain text link reading "Listed on LaunchNest" instead of loading the badge, so the page does not request anything from launchnest.io.
  • For visitors outside those regions, a lightweight badge image may load from launchnest.io as configured. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the badge image is not loaded.

Videos from YouTube

Some pages show short KuickKonvert videos that are hosted on YouTube, a service of Google. Until you act, the page shows only a still image stored on our own server, and nothing is requested from YouTube or Google for the video.

  • For visitors in the Consent-Controlled Locations, we do not load the YouTube player. The image is a link that opens the video on YouTube in a new tab, only if you choose to follow it.
  • For other visitors, a video plays on the page only if you press the button labelled "Play Video — Loads player from YouTube (Google). Connects to external servers and processes device details." The player then loads from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Google receives your IP address and technical information about your browser and device, and may set cookies or similar storage entries, under Google's Privacy Policy. According to Google, views in privacy-enhanced mode are not used to personalise your YouTube experience or the advertising you see elsewhere. Each video also has a link to watch it on YouTube instead.

Transfers to Google in the United States

When Google tags load (after your consent, where consent is required) or when you play or open a video, information such as your IP address goes to Google LLC in the United States. Google states that Google LLC and its wholly-owned US subsidiaries have certified adherence to the Data Privacy Framework Principles, and that for its ads and analytics services it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, in certain circumstances, the UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (Google: international data transfers). Your uploaded files are never sent to Google: they are processed on our server in Singapore and deleted as described above.

Which of the following rights you have, and on what terms, depends on the law that applies to you and its conditions. They include the rights to:

  • have inaccurate personal data corrected;
  • have your personal data deleted;

Because we do not run user accounts, most of the data described above is not linked to your name, and we may not be able to find it unless you give us details that identify it, such as the date, time and IP address of your visit, or your Google Analytics client ID. We may ask for information to confirm that the request is yours. Some rights apply only in certain circumstances or are subject to legal exemptions, so not every request can be granted in full; if we cannot meet a request, we will tell you why. We will reply within one month of receiving your request; where the law allows, we may extend this by up to two further months for complex or numerous requests, and we will tell you if we do. For data that Google holds, you can also use the Google links in this notice.
