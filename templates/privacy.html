Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 October 2026

KuickKonvert ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website KuickKonvert.com, providing file conversion and PDF utility tools. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website.

What we do with your files

Files you upload to KuickKonvert are used only to perform the conversion you requested. Each upload is processed in its own temporary, isolated workspace on our server -- including any working files the converter creates along the way -- and that workspace is automatically and permanently deleted as soon as your converted file is returned to you, or if the conversion fails. If a server process stops unexpectedly in the middle of a conversion, any workspace it leaves behind is removed automatically by a later clean-up. We do not review, copy, back up, or retain your files.

What we don't do

We do not store your files after conversion. We do not use uploaded file contents to train any model. We do not sell or share file contents with third parties.

Technical logs and IP addresses

Like any website, our server and our hosting provider see your IP address and basic request details -- such as the page or tool requested, the time and the response code -- in order to deliver pages and protect the service; our hosting providers keep their own logs under their own policies. We use IP addresses to apply fair-use limits on how many pages and conversions can be requested per minute, per hour and per day; these counters are held in memory for no longer than 24 hours. Our own application logs record which tool was used and technical error details when a conversion fails. They never contain file contents, and they are designed to leave out file names, although a report of an unexpected error may occasionally include one.

Where your files are processed

KuickKonvert runs on servers operated by our hosting provider, Render, in its Singapore (Southeast Asia) region, and traffic reaches those servers through Cloudflare's network. When you upload a file, it is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to that server, converted there, and deleted as described above. If you are outside Singapore, this means your file is briefly processed in another country.

Your responsibility

Please do not upload files containing information you are not authorised to share (e.g. third-party confidential data) unless you have the right to process it this way.

1. Cookies and Similar Storage

KuickKonvert’s own functional code does not set any tracking cookies. However, when you visit our site, our consent management system and third-party measurement tools (such as Google Analytics and Google Ads) may place cookies or local-storage entries on your device, provided you have given appropriate consent.

Your Consent Choices

We store your preferences regarding analytics and advertising cookies in your browser’s local storage under the key kk_consent. This record contains:

  • Your choice for Analytics (accepted or rejected)
  • Your choice for Advertising (accepted or rejected)
  • The date and time your choice was recorded
  • The version of the consent message accepted

This consent record stays entirely in your browser and is not transmitted to us.

2. Google Analytics

If you consent to Analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve our conversion tools and user experience.

Automatically Generated Events: Google Analytics may record automatically generated events, such as page views, scrolling to a point near the end of a page, and user engagement. The specific events collected depend on our Google Analytics configuration.

Tool & Form Interactions: GA4 may track custom interactions to help us measure tool utility (such as when a tool page is viewed or when a conversion utility is initiated).

Absolute Privacy Assurance: Our Google Analytics events are designed not to include your uploaded files, their filenames, or their contents. We test our event payloads to check that this information is not transmitted. None of our analytics payloads include personal files, document text, or entered values.

3. Google Ads

If you consent to Advertising, we use Google Ads tags to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns and understand how visitors arrive at KuickKonvert.

Ad-Click Identifiers: Page addresses and titles may include ad-click identifiers (such as gclid) when you arrive via an advertisement.

Remarketing & Personalisation: When permitted by your consent settings, Google Ads may utilize remarketing signals to deliver relevant advertising experiences.

Conversion Tracking: We use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure conversions attributed to eligible interactions with our ads, according to configured attribution settings.

4. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are accessing KuickKonvert from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, no Google tags or third-party measurement scripts will load until you explicitly provide your consent via our consent banner.

Consent States & Behavior

  • No Choice Yet / Reject All: Google tags are not loaded, and no Google network requests are sent.
  • Analytics Only: Google Analytics is loaded to measure site performance; advertising storage and remarketing remain disabled.
  • Advertising Only: Google Ads tags are loaded; analytics collection remains inactive.
  • Accept All: Both categories are enabled according to your preferences.

We will ask for your preferences again after 12 months, or sooner if we materially update our consent management practices or processing categories.

Withdrawing or Changing Your Consent

You can change your choice at any time by clicking Cookie settings in the footer of any page.

If you withdraw consent for Analytics or Advertising:

  • We update your saved choice in local storage and remove the relevant cookies and storage entries that our site is able to clear.
  • We reload the page where necessary so that the corresponding tags stop executing immediately.
  • The category for which you have withdrawn consent will not be loaded on subsequent pages. Any category you continue to allow may remain active according to your updated preferences.

Please note: Our site cannot remove information that Google stores under its own domains or information Google has already received prior to withdrawal.

Visitors outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

We do not currently show a consent banner to visitors from other regions. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

5. Third-Party Content (LaunchNest Badge)

To maintain strict privacy and performance standards:

  • For visitors in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, we display a plain text link reading "Listed on LaunchNest" instead of loading embedded widgets, ensuring no requests are made to launchnest.io.

Advertising on this site

KuickKonvert may display third-party advertisements (for example, through Google AdSense) to help cover hosting costs, keeping every conversion tool free to use. If ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may set and read cookies, use web beacons and receive your IP address to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can review or adjust your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com, and you can also control cookies generally through your browser's settings. We do not control the cookies set by third-party ad providers directly.
