About KuickKonvert

What KuickKonvert is

KuickKonvert — spelled with a K

KuickKonvert is a free online file conversion service covering Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, and image formats -- {{ tool_count }} tools in total, from everyday conversions like Word to PDF through to PDF utilities like merging, splitting, compressing, and password-protecting. Every tool works in your web browser, with your file converted on our server and then deleted, so there's nothing to install and no account to create.

Alongside the tools, our guides explain in plain English what actually changes when you convert a file -- fonts, page layout, file size, password protection and more -- based on our own tests.

How it's built

Three principles shape every tool on this site:

  • No sign-up. Every tool is usable immediately -- there's no account, no email capture, and no login wall between you and your file.
  • Nothing is kept. Each upload is processed in an isolated, temporary workspace and deleted automatically once your converted file is returned to you (or immediately if the conversion fails). See the Privacy notice for the full details.
  • Free to use. Every conversion tool on KuickKonvert is free, with a 50MB limit per upload (all files together) to keep conversions fast for everyone.

How we test

Before a change goes live, we run it on a test copy of the site and convert sample files with every tool, then check the results. The test results in our guides come from our own conversions of test files, and those guides say what we tested and with which software. Your own files may behave differently, so always check a converted file before you rely on it.
