About KuickKonvert

What KuickKonvert is

KuickKonvert is a free online file conversion service covering Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, and image formats -- 16 tools in total, from everyday conversions like Word to PDF through to PDF utilities like merging, splitting, compressing, and password-protecting. Every tool runs directly from your browser: there's nothing to install and no account to create.

How it's built

Three principles shape every tool on this site:

  • No sign-up. Every tool is usable immediately -- there's no account, no email capture, and no login wall between you and your file.
  • Nothing is stored. Each upload is processed in an isolated, temporary workspace and deleted automatically once your converted file is returned to you (or immediately if the conversion fails). See the Privacy notice for the full details.
  • Free to use. Every conversion tool on KuickKonvert is free, with a 50MB per-file limit to keep conversions fast for everyone.

Who operates KuickKonvert

KuickKonvert is a production of Shahid Iqbal. For questions, feedback, or support, see the Contact page.
