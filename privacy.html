What we do with your files

Files you upload to KuickKonvert are used only to perform the conversion you requested. Each file is processed in a temporary, isolated workspace and is automatically and permanently deleted as soon as your converted file is returned to you, or if the conversion fails. We do not review, copy, back up, or retain your files.

What we don't do

We do not store your files after conversion. We do not use uploaded file contents to train any model. We do not sell or share file contents with third parties.

Basic technical logs

Our servers may keep standard operational logs (timestamps, error codes, approximate file size) to diagnose failures and prevent abuse. These logs do not include file contents.

Your responsibility

Please do not upload files containing information you are not authorised to share (e.g. third-party confidential data) unless you have the right to process it this way.
